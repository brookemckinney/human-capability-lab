# Human Capability Lab

*A public research space exploring how people develop judgment, capability, and organizational intelligence in the age of AI.*

---

## Purpose

Artificial intelligence has changed how work is created.

This repository explores a different question:

> **How do humans continue developing capability when intelligent systems can increasingly generate the work we once used as evidence of learning and expertise?**

Human Capability Lab is a collection of ongoing research, experiments, frameworks, prototypes, and practical resources focused on that question.

---

## Current Areas of Research

### Human Judgment

How people evaluate information, uncertainty, and competing possibilities.

### Organizational Learning

How organizations develop collective intelligence rather than individual expertise alone.

### AI Enablement

Helping teams integrate AI without diminishing human responsibility.

### Assessment

Designing better ways to recognize thinking when outputs are no longer reliable evidence.

### Wonder

Understanding the relational conditions that allow people to notice, question, and create.

## Research Principles

- Human judgment matters.
- Better thinking depends on better environments.
- AI should augment—not replace—human capability.
- Organizations become more intelligent when people feel safe enough to think aloud.
