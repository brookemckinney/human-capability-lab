# DECIDE

DECIDE is a human-centered framework for making responsible decisions in AI-supported environments.

Its purpose is to help people slow down consequential decisions long enough to examine context, evidence, uncertainty, tradeoffs, and responsibility.

## Core Premise

Artificial intelligence can assist with analysis and generate possible courses of action.

It cannot determine which human values should matter most or assume responsibility for the outcome.

## Decision Questions

Before acting on an AI-supported recommendation, consider:

- What decision is actually being made?
- What evidence supports the recommendation?
- What important context is missing?
- Which assumptions are shaping the output?
- Who is affected by the decision?
- What tradeoffs are being accepted?
- What remains uncertain?
- Who owns the final decision?

## Appropriate Uses

DECIDE may support:

- organizational strategy
- AI governance
- leadership decisions
- instructional planning
- policy review
- workflow design
- product decisions
- high-stakes communication

## Human Responsibility

AI may help people compare options, identify patterns, or surface risks.

Humans remain responsible for:

- defining the problem
- determining relevant values
- evaluating evidence
- interpreting context
- selecting a course of action
- accepting accountability

## Guiding Principle

> AI may generate possibilities. Humans remain responsible for framing, evaluating, choosing, and acting.
