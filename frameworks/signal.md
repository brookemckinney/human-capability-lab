# SIGNAL

SIGNAL is a practical lens for examining whether AI-supported work provides meaningful evidence of human thinking, judgment, and contribution.

## Purpose

AI-generated outputs can be fluent, polished, and convincing while still concealing weak reasoning or limited human involvement.

SIGNAL helps reviewers look beyond the surface quality of an artifact.

## The Central Question

> What does this work actually signal about the human capability behind it?

## Areas of Examination

SIGNAL can be used to examine:

- the quality of the original problem framing
- the relevance of the context provided
- the evidence used
- the decisions made
- the revisions completed
- the judgment exercised
- the limitations recognized
- the responsibility accepted

## Use Cases

SIGNAL may be applied to:

- student work
- employee deliverables
- AI-assisted reports
- hiring portfolios
- organizational proposals
- strategic recommendations
- professional learning
- performance evaluation

## Guiding Principle

A polished artifact is not always evidence of polished thinking.

The reviewer must examine the signals surrounding the work, not merely the final product.
